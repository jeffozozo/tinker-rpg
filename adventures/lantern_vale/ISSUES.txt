Enemy rewards now drop as ordinary loot at the enemy’s death position. Walk

BELL OF DAWN — NEW PLAYTEST ISSUES
November 13, 2026

These are observations for review, not changes to the engine. The previous
adventure and its issue report were replaced as requested. Coordinates below
are zero-based authoring coordinates. Normal gameplay does not print them.

1. Story messages can appear BEFORE the action that caused them.
Observed during the full native runner playthrough. Enemy reward dialogue says
"The guardian crumbles" before the final attack, "Moss Guardian falls", and the
inventory reward. The donation thanks precedes "You pay $5". The ending precedes
"You no longer carry the red gem".
Reproduce: defeat the Moss Guardian, pay the optional castle donation, or return
the crimson heart. See playthrough.txt for the exact ordering.
Cause: story dialogs invoke the UI callback immediately, while action reports
wait in engine.events until the runner's post-action flush.
Impact: combat, rewards and the ending read out of order.
Possible direction for discussion: queue all adventure text in action order.

2. Finite stat triggers spend charges when nothing is restored.
Reproduce in a fresh game: reach Briarford's runestone at (10,4) with full energy.
The two-charge energy trigger goes from 2 charges to 1 although energy stays
10/10 and no restoration message appears. Leaving and returning at full energy
can exhaust the shrine without benefiting from it.
Confirmed in verification.json using an isolated fresh snapshot.
The normal playthrough deliberately spent energy before using each charge,
then confirmed the exhausted trigger stays exhausted rather than becoming
unlimited. This is a semantics question: should a clamped, zero-effect stat
change count as a successful use?

3. Deferred enemy rewards need a clearer recovery cue.
With a full inventory, defeat a moving enemy with an attached Inventory reward.
The safe behavior works: the reward stays pending, survives save/load, and can
be collected after dropping an item. However, the message says only:
"Your inventory is full. Drop something on open ground, then return to collect
this item."
In the isolated test, the guardian died at (6,7), but its fixed reward trigger
remained at (6,4). Returning to where it died does not recover the reward; the
player must walk onto the original trigger location. There is no visible marker
for that pending reward. The following reward dialogue remains pending too.
This follows the agreed fixed-position trigger design; the issue is discoverability,
not lost inventory. Consider an in-world cue or clearer recovery wording without
changing where triggers are anchored.

4. Open-door tiles default to blocked.
Confirmed while authoring and testing the optional donation paving:
"gray_brick_open_door" with walkable_override=None cannot be entered.
The castle's outside doorway would also be blocked without an override.
Cause: default_tile_walkable recognizes "_door_", but these asset names end
with "_door". They are classified as unknown rather than doors.
Adventure workaround: explicitly mark door tiles walkable using the editor's
Walkable control. Trigger-opened gates also set walkable=True. The game now
works with those overrides; the shared default logic was left for review.

AUTHORING REFINEMENTS ALREADY MADE
- Put enemy warnings on arrival tiles so ranged kills cannot precede the warning.
- Put the final throne off the through-route so normal exploration does not
  repeatedly attempt to spend the final quest item.
- Use an optional donation instead of a shop/toll transaction. A failed Funds
  trigger stops subsequent actions but does not physically prevent walking on.
- Shrine text describes two uses without promising unsupported visual flame changes.
