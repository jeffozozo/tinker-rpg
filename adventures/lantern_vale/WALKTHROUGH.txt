UPDATE — November 16, 2026: Enemy-trigger attachments have been removed.
Enemy rewards now drop as ordinary loot at the enemy’s death position. Walk
onto that loot to collect it. The castle armory purse is a separate walk-on
pickup. References to attached/pending defeat rewards below are historical.

SPOILERS — THE BELL OF DAWN

1. Dawnmere: walk north to Mara. Collect the sword, book and torch along the
   crossroad. Equip the sword and torch in inventory; read the book and turn
   the torch on. The fountain southeast restores health. Leave south.
2. Vale: head east to Briarford, fighting the slime and spider with F. Blocked
   moves take turns too. Return to Dawnmere to heal if needed.
3. Briarford: collect and equip helmet, shield and wand. Collect the blue gem.
   Speak to Fen in the northeast. The candles' runestone has TWO energy
   recharges; avoid using it at full energy. A purse in the northwest awards $12.
4. Grove: return west and take the northwest stairs. Defeat the Moss Guardian
   for the green seal. The stone figure southwest increases strength once.
   Red water southeast hurts. Use the blue gem when hurt or low on energy to
   free a slot before accepting the seal. Leave south.
5. Castle: take the northeast road from the Vale. The keeper's $5 restoration
   donation is optional. Step just south of the inner wall with the green seal
   to open the gate. The lever in the west reveals a restoration gem. The sentry
   guards the hall. The throne is northwest; the belfry stairs are northeast.
   Save here if you like. Return to a village for healing or remaining recharges
   if needed. Clear inventory space for the final reward.
6. Belfry: defeat The Hush. Ranged wand attacks work down the passage; use your
   sword if energy runs out. The crimson heart is awarded on defeat. If your
   inventory is full, free a slot and return to its original trigger location
   in the narrow passage (5,5), not necessarily where the enemy died.
7. Return down the stairs to the castle. Walk in front of the northwest throne
   with the crimson heart. The spring clears, the bell rings, and you win.

Drop demonstration: use inventory's Drop on the book while on clear ground,
walk away, then walk back to recover it. Equipment and light state survive saves.
On death the Game Over prompt can restart the adventure with a fresh world.
